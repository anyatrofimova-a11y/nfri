Who carries non-firm power risk — and who is prepared to underwrite it.

An outside-in index of the UK insurance-market participants exposed to interruptible-power risk across energy assets and data centres. Two axes, one number, every rating sourced.

Margin of Safety = Preparedness − Exposure. The market prices capacity onto whoever looks exposed, but losses concentrate where exposure runs ahead of preparedness — while the genuinely capable sit in under-deployed whitespace.

Method & evals

Scoring is arithmetic in code, never an LLM opinion (harness/scoring.py); no value is synthetic (contract/DATA_POLICY.md). The harness grades itself L0–L8 — L5 is the publication gate.

Ratings fuse latent (sourced research judgement) and deterministic (register/filing) inputs by credibility weighting, r_eff = clamp(λ·r_det + (1−λ)·r_lat) (contract/MODEL_SPEC.md). Quadrants use in-sample median cut-lines. Every rating links to its source; assessed values are marked; anything below the publication gate is PROVISIONAL. This is an outside-in research aid — not audited positions, not investment advice.

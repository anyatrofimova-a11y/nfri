- Broker playbook: utility-failure NDBI triggers, CBI Tier-1 energy suppliers, **parametric grid outage duration** layers.
- DC exposure resembles **energy/utility risk** (transformers, load imbalance) not just square-footage tier classification.
- Underwriters want **energy dependency maps** and contractual risk structures, not legacy certifications.
